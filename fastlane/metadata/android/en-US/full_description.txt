The Bomb: every screenshot lands armed. Defuse the keepers, detonate the rest.

The moment you take a screenshot, a compact popup floats over your screen —
thumbnail, filename, and your choices: Defuse it forever, Archive it for
tonight, or Detonate now. No digging through your gallery. No forgotten
screenshots piling up for months.

FUSES THAT FIT YOUR LIFE
Don't want to decide now? Light a fuse. Quick presets — 10 minutes, 1 hour,
1 day, 1 week — or a slider for any custom duration. Every timed screenshot
wears a live countdown chip; cut the fuse any time before it fires.

THE NIGHTLY BLAST
Archived screenshots get one last review window. At your chosen time, all
archived, un-defused, un-fused screenshots are cleared. The Home screen
always shows when the next blast runs, and can warn you 30 minutes ahead.

DEFUSED = SAFE FOREVER
Defused screenshots are exempt from every fuse and every blast. A permanent
vault, one tab away.

AFTERMATH, MEASURED
Animated stats: processed, storage cleared, detonated vs defused, with a
live breakdown of armed, archived, and safe.

FIND ANYTHING
Search by filename, filter by date range.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN SIGHT
A built-in dashboard shows exactly which permissions are granted and which
aren't, with one-tap shortcuts to fix any of them.

PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE
No accounts. No analytics. No ads. The app doesn't even request the INTERNET
permission — it is technically incapable of phoning home. Everything stays on
your device, and every deletion passes through Android's own confirmation
dialog. Free and open source under the MIT license.

MATERIAL YOU
Dynamic color on Android 12+, a hand-crafted palette on older devices, full
dark mode, and a small celebration when your triage list hits zero.
