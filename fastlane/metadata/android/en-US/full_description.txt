The Bomb: every screenshot lands armed. Defuse the keepers, detonate the
rest — on your schedule, on your device.

TRIAGE IN ONE TAP
The moment a screenshot lands, a translucent popup (and a notification with
thumbnail) appears: Defuse, Archive, Detonate Now — or light a fuse without
leaving the popup.

FUSES THAT ACTUALLY FIT
Four quick fuse presets, each fully yours to configure — 10 minutes, 1 hour,
1 day, 1 week by default, changeable to any amount of minutes, hours, days,
or weeks. Prefer something else? A custom slider picks the exact duration
with a live "Detonates in…" preview. Timed screenshots wear a live countdown
chip; cut the fuse any time before it fires.

THE DAILY BLAST
Archive what you're not sure about. At your chosen time (default 11:30 PM)
all archived, un-defused, un-fused screenshots are detonated — with an
optional heads-up 30 minutes before. The Home screen always shows when the
next blast runs.

DEFUSED = SAFE FOREVER
Pinned screenshots are exempt from every fuse and the daily blast. No
exceptions.

STATS THAT FEEL GOOD
Animated counters and a fallout report: screenshots processed, storage
cleared, detonated vs defused.

FIND ANYTHING
Search by filename and filter by date range.

PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE
No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No network permission at all — the app
cannot phone home because it has no way to. Everything stays on your device,
and Android's own system dialog approves every deletion.

MATERIAL YOU
Dynamic color on Android 12+, a hand-crafted teal/amber palette on older
devices, full dark mode, serif-and-sans typography, and a small celebration
when your triage list hits zero.
